Data source

BITRE Hospitalised Injury Data, from the AIHW National Hospital Morbidity Database (ICD-10-AM coding). Coverage: 2011–2021, cleaned in KNIME.

Data notes

  • Series breaks: Victoria (2012) and NSW (2017) show shifts.
  • Suppressed cells: small counts marked "n.p." are preserved.
  • Missing fields: "Died in care" is omitted for 2020–2021.
  • Data lag: the most recent national release is 2021.
